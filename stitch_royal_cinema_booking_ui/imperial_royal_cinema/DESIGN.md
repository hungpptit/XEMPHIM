## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-exclusive, private cinema lounge atmosphere modeled after bespoke European royal screening rooms and 5-star premier screening clubs. It establishes a mood of uncompromising luxury, discretion, and prestige. Every touchpoint feels tactile, ceremonial, and opulent, evoking the sensory drama of dimming auditorium chandeliers, brushed golden fixtures, and heavy velvet finishes.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Royal Dark Skeuomorphism** with modern **Luminous Glassmorphism**. Dark surfaces are rendered in obsidian and deep onyx tones, accented with directional metallic gold reflections, brushed multi-stop metal gradients, and warm champagne halos. The user is treated not as a casual moviegoer, but as an honored patron entering a private screening sanctuary.

## Colors

The color system operates strictly in dark mode, anchoring all contrast in deep abyss foundations to magnify golden specular highlights:

- **Obsidian Black (`#08090C`)**: Serves as the primary stage canvas, creating cinematic black levels that allow golden lighting and film posters to pop with maximum drama.
- **Onyx Surface (`#12161F` / `rgba(18, 22, 31, 0.85)`)**: Used for layered cards, floating sheets, and glassmorphic dialogs, distinguishing spatial tiers without breaking the dark mood.
- **Gold Hairline (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`)**: The unifying border signature across all modules, providing metallic definition without visual clutter.
- **Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`) & Champagne Gold (`#F5E6AB`)**: The core brand accents. Imperial Gold drives buttons, primary icons, and selected boundaries; Champagne Gold elevates display text and hover luminescence.
- **Antique Bronze (`#AA771C`) & Deep Gold (`#B8860B`)**: Provide deep anchoring stops within metallic gradients to simulate brushed reflective foil.

### Seat Status Palette
The cinema auditorium seat matrix requires unambiguous functional status mapping:
- **Available (`#D1D5DB`)**: Satin Silver, subtly luminescent against dark seating carpets.
- **VIP Patron (`#F3C644`)**: Crown-embossed Imperial Gold, reserved for premium lounge recliners.
- **Selected (`#FFD700`)**: Bright Gold paired with an active outer specular halo.
- **Locked / Pending Checkout (`#FF8C00`)**: Amber Glow, pulsing over a 120-second dynamic release lock.
- **Occupied / Sold (`#2A2E39`)**: Muted Charcoal, recessed into the dark ground to minimize visual distraction.

## Typography

The typographic strategy contrasts classic royal serif prestige with ultra-clean modern geometric clarity:

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs sharp serifs and high stroke contrast to present film titles and auditorium billing. High-tier headers utilize text-fill clipping with a tri-color metallic gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 20%, #D4AF37 70%, #AA771C 100%)`.
- **System, Timers & Controls (`Outfit`)**: Manages the data-dense booking interface. Its open apertures and modern geometry maintain razor-sharp legibility for movie showtimes, monetary sums, countdown tickers, and seat row numbers.
- **Kerning & Styling**: Hero titles, button CTAs, and metadata tags require elevated letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.12em`) and uppercase transformation to project an exclusive, architectural presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt modular spacing grid** designed to accommodate complex interactive seating plans and streamlined checkout flows:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1360px)**: 
  - Standard gutters are `1.5rem` with `3rem` screen margins.
  - The checkout process splits into an asymmetrical two-column showcase: 60% left column for the active ticket itinerary and theater seat ledger, 40% right column locked with the metallic QR code payment terminal.
- **Tablet & Mobile (4 to 8 Columns)**:
  - Responsive margins compress to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters.
  - The seat selection flow activates a fixed, glassmorphic bottom drawer (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) that docks ticket tallies and primary confirmation CTAs directly above phone navigation.
- **Seat Cluster Grid Rhythm**:
  - Seats maintain a `0.375rem` (`6px`) standard intra-cluster gap.
  - Center and lateral pedestrian aisles feature a dedicated `1.25rem` (`20px`) breathing buffer to clearly demarcate seating sectors (e.g., 4–4–4 layout).

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on translucent glass planes and ambient gold luminescence rather than generic neutral shadows:

1. **Base Level (Canvas)**: Solid Obsidian Black (`#08090C`). Zero elevation, non-reflective backdrop.
2. **Surface Tier (Cards & Containers)**: Semi-translucent Onyx (`#12161F`) framed by a subtle 1px metallic border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`). Dropshadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
3. **Floating Glass Tier (Checkout Drawers, Modals, Dynamic Panels)**: 
   - Fill: `rgba(18, 22, 31, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
   - Border: 1px hairline `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`.
   - Ambient Rim Shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
4. **Cinematic Ambient Screen Glow**:
   - The curved cinema screen top arch incorporates a downward diffused gold projection: `box-shadow: 0px 12px 45px -5px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45); border-top: 3px solid #D4AF37;`. This simulates light bouncing off projector linen onto the premier seating tiers.
5. **Interactive Selection Specular**:
   - Active seats radiate a 360-degree golden luminescence: `box-shadow: 0 0 15px #FFD700;`.
   - Expiring seat holds pulse with an amber warning field: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 140, 0, 0.60);`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Roundedness Level 2 (Rounded)**, blending geometric luxury with bespoke vintage theater elements:

- **Cards and Panels**: Base radius of `0.5rem` (8px); modals, ticket cards, and major container shells scale up to `1rem` (16px) for an intentional, polished contour.
- **Seat Icons**: Square modules with minimal softened corners (`4px` / `0.25rem`), preserving structural density within seat matrices while softening tactile contact.
- **Pill Filters & Badges**: Date selectors, projection formats (IMAX, Gold Class), and hall badges use a fully rounded capsule (`9999px`) profile to simulate polished brass tokens.
- **Vintage Boarding-Pass Notches**: Ticket confirmation modules incorporate opposing semi-circular cutouts (16px diameter) along horizontal divider lines, creating a tactile physical stub silhouette.
- **Ornamental Brackets**: High-value focal points—such as the dynamic payment QR module—feature four custom metallic L-shaped corner brackets instead of standard card corners, referencing classical royal invitation frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Imperial CTA (*"ĐẶT VÉ NGAY"*, *"THANH TOÁN"*)**: 
  - Background: Multi-stop reflective gold gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #F5E6AB 50%, #B8860B 100%)`.
  - Text: Obsidian Black (`#08090C`), uppercase `label-cta`, bold.
  - Shape: Rounded capsule (`9999px`) or `0.5rem` structural radius.
  - Hover / Focus: Scaled specular glow `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.60); transform: translateY(-1px);`.
- **Secondary Ghost CTA**:
  - Background: Transparent with `rgba(18, 22, 31, 0.60)` glass tint.
  - Border: 1px hairline `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
  - Text: Champagne Gold (`#F5E6AB`).

### Seat Selector Grid
- Dimension: `28px × 28px` base desktop dimension; `22px × 22px` mobile dimension.
- **Standard Available**: Solid Satin Silver (`#D1D5DB`) outline with dark fill; shifts to full silver fill on hover.
- **VIP Lounge**: Imperial Gold (`#F3C644`) with an etched miniature crown icon (`👑`).
- **Selected**: Solid Bright Gold (`#FFD700`) with an intense specular halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 15px #FFD700`).
- **Occupied**: Muted Charcoal (`#2A2E39`), low contrast, unclickable cursor.
- **Temporary Lock (120s Countdown)**: Amber Glow (`#FF8C00`) with a rhythmic 1.5s pulse animation.

### Date & Showtime Chips
- **Inactive**: Onyx background (`#12161F`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`), Silver Smoke text (`#9CA3AF`).
- **Active / Selected**: Gradient border (`#D4AF37`), subtle golden ambient under-glow, pure white primary text with bold champagne time highlights.

### Movie & Ticket Cards
- **Movie Poster Card**: Framed with a 1px gold hairline. On hover, the border illuminates to solid `#D4AF37`, image scale transitions smoothly (`scale-105`), and a bottom gold fog overlay reveals genres and booking triggers.
- **VIP Boarding Pass (Ticket Detail)**: Dark onyx glass container split horizontally by a dashed gold foil divider line (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`), finished with bilateral half-circle cutouts.

### Dynamic QR Payment Module
- Centered high-contrast QR matrix bordered by an Onyx backdrop.
- Embellished with four Imperial Gold corner brackets (`#D4AF37`).
- Features a live payment-polling heartbeat indicator (`🔄`) with an automated 2000ms query cadence and a dynamic countdown timer (`⏱️`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form elements use dark onyx background fills (`#12161F`) with recessed inner shadows.
- Active focus state triggers a crisp 1px Gold Hairline border and a soft Champagne blur (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
